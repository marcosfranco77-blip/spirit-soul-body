Spirit, Soul and Body

Our being is made of three parts

✦ ✦ ✦
Now may the God of peace himself sanctify you completely, and may your whole spirit, soul, and body be kept blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus, the Messiah. 1 Thessalonians 5:23  ·  "your whole being" — ISV
For the word of God is living and powerful, and sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing even to the division of soul and spirit, and of joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart. Hebrews 4:12

A The three parts of our being

A.1 The body

The body is very obvious: you only have to look at yourself in a mirror. It works through the five senses: sight, hearing, smell, taste, and touch.

In itself it is a very complex organism
  • It is controlled chemically and electrically by the brain.
  • It handles many functions automatically.
  • It has reflexes in response to actions.
  • Some functions already came with us before we were born, built into the brain.
  • Others need a process of learning to be controlled, and are then recorded in the brain to function autonomously.

A.2 The soul

You cannot see the soul, but you can touch it with words or images. When you speak to me, you speak to my soul. It is my:

  • mind
  • intellect
  • will
  • emotions
  • desires
  • intentions
  • conscience

The conscience is part of the soul. It was given by God to "all men," to reveal all ungodliness and unrighteousness.

For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men, who suppress the truth in unrighteousness, because what may be known of God is manifest in them, for God has shown it to them. Romans 1:18-19
Now the Spirit expressly says that in latter times some will depart from the faith, giving heed to deceiving spirits and doctrines of demons, speaking lies in hypocrisy, having their own conscience seared. 1 Timothy 4:1-2

The soul can also be called personality (the mental-emotional part).

A.3 The flesh: body + soul

We can call the body and the soul together the flesh.

Can I touch your flesh? Yes!

  • Body: by pinching you. You can see it in a mirror.
  • Soul: I can touch you with words and make you happy, sad, angry; I can hurt you.

There is a deep connection between the body and the soul:

  • A sick mind can make the body sick.
  • A sick body can make the mind, emotions, and will sick.
Conclusion In the natural, you are in contact with other people only through your body and your soul. This is how the world in general communicates and lives their lives.

A.4 The spirit

The spirit is a completely different matter. There is no direct connection between the flesh and the spirit.

That which is born of the flesh is flesh, and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit. John 3:6

Jesus taught about two different births.

Unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God. John 3:5

Jesus is referring to a second birth that is NOT physical, but spiritual.

Example 1 · The three-step ladder

Imagine a ladder with three steps. Most people interact between steps #1 (body) and #2 (soul).

Do not try to make them understand the spiritual truths of step #3: that dimension does not exist for them. Their spirit is dead, separated from God, from His Word, from the spiritual world, and from spiritual truths.

There is no connection with the Holy Spirit who reveals truth, so they cannot perceive spiritual reality, unless they receive Christ.

Example 2 · The paper and the cube

Imagine a sheet of paper and a cube, side by side. A being living inside the paper can only look forward, backward, and to both sides, but cannot look up: there is no "up" for it.

Whoever lives inside the cube can do the same as the one in the paper, but can also look up.

For the born-again believer, the spirit is the part through which:

  • God communicates with him,
  • and through which the Life and Power of God flow.
The body without the spirit is dead. James 2:26

Of your three parts (body, soul, and spirit), the part that is giving life within you is your spirit.

B The spirit and the natural

B.1 Not perceived by the natural

  • The spirit CANNOT be felt, seen, or touched; only the soul or the body can be touched.
  • You cannot access your spirit from the natural.
  • Your flesh cannot reveal anything to you about your spirit.

B.2 The natural mind cannot discern the spiritual

The natural world was created by the spiritual world. The natural world cannot discern or describe something it is not part of.

Example · The computer It is as if a computer tried to discover and describe by itself who created it; to discern the human being. This information is not within the computer, unless the human being reveals it to it.

The false religions of the world are inventions of the soul, in an attempt to discern the spiritual world from the intellect and emotions. Much like a computer trying to describe its creator.

But the soul can never discern the spiritual; therefore, they are human attempts trying to describe knowledge that is not within them.

The spiritual world needs to be revealed by the spiritual world. Even though God has put eternity within us, the knowledge of the spiritual is not within us: it needs to be revealed by the Holy Spirit through the Word of God.

He [God] has put eternity in their hearts, except that no one can find out the work that God does, from beginning to end. Ecclesiastes 3:11

B.3 In the natural, you only trust a mirror

You do not go out into the street:

  • "feeling" that you look good,
  • "feeling" that your face is clean,
  • "feeling" that your hair is well combed,
  • "feeling" that your clothes look good.

You need a mirror to:

  • trust what you see,
  • look at how you appear,
  • fix whatever you need to fix,
  • and then go out.

You have never seen your face directly: you have seen it only through a mirror. To look at your spirit you also need to look at yourself in a mirror, but a completely different mirror.

C Two completely different mirrors

C.1 The natural mirror and the spiritual mirror

Just as the natural mirror works for your body, the Word of God is the spiritual mirror to look at your spirit. In the same way:

  • The Word of God is the mirror to see what your spirit is like.
  • You can discover your spirit only through this mirror.
  • In the Word you can discover your new identity.
  • It describes who you really are.
  • It is the perfect reflection of spiritual truth.
  • The life and power of God are already placed in you; they only need to begin to flow toward your soul and your body.

C.2 The mirror of the spirit

Where do we get this idea of the mirror?

For if anyone is a hearer of the word and not a doer, he is like a man observing his natural face in a mirror; for he observes himself, goes away, and immediately forgets what kind of man he was. But he who looks into the perfect law of liberty and continues in it, and is not a forgetful hearer but a doer of the work, this one will be blessed in what he does. James 1:23-25

C.3 It's already in your spirit!

The Bible reveals spiritual reality to you, in the same way as looking into a spiritual mirror, and by faith, receiving what is already in your spirit.

By the revelation of the Holy Spirit, the Word is the only way to see the perfect image of who you are in the spirit.

It is the Spirit who gives life; the flesh profits nothing. The words that I speak to you are spirit, and they are life. John 6:63

D Your spirit is a new creation

Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; old things have passed away [past tense]; behold, all things have become new. Now all things are of God [all this is a gift from God], who has reconciled us to Himself through Jesus Christ. 2 Corinthians 5:17-18

D.1 What was created new?

When you received Christ:

  • Your body did not change when you were saved (lazy, sick, overweight?).
  • Your soul did not change (sad, depressed, defeated).
  • If you were depressed before being saved, you may continue depressed immediately afterward.

What was created new? By process of elimination:

  • It was not the body.
  • It was not the soul.
  • The only part of your being left is your spirit.

D.2 Your spirit was created new

There was an instant transformation when you received Christ.

And you He made alive… God, who is rich in mercy, because of His great love with which He loved us, even when we were dead in trespasses, made us alive together with Christ (by grace you have been saved), and raised us up together, and made us sit together in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus. Ephesians 2:1, 4-6 — NKJV

The only way the verse above can be true is that a new creation took place in your spirit.

[God] has blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places in Christ. Ephesians 1:3

And the only way we can be seated in heavenly places is with our spirit.

❱ "Made us sit together in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus" — see Ephesians 2:6 (above)

Upon receiving Christ, through His death and resurrection, your old spirit dies and is born again. God gave you a new spirit, identical to the Spirit of Christ, and within your new spirit the Holy Spirit dwells forever. God communicates with you, and you with Him, through your spirit.

D.3 The "Spirit of Christ" and the "Holy Spirit"

The Spirit of Christ Upon accepting Jesus as Savior, your spirit is born again, becoming a new creature, with a new nature: the nature of Christ. That is why we have the "Spirit of Christ." Your new spirit was created in the image of the Spirit of Christ.
The Holy Spirit He is the third person of the Trinity, who comes to dwell in you and to empower you, guiding you into all truth, glorifying Christ, leading you into an intimate relationship with Jesus, to show you things to come, enabling you to live a victorious Christian life.

Our "new spirit" is ONE with the "Spirit of Jesus"

By the power of the Holy Spirit, a born-again person has the Spirit of Christ in him.

Now if anyone does not have the Spirit of Christ, he is not His. And if Christ is in you, the body is dead because of sin. Romans 8:9b-10

There is a spiritual union between Christ and the believer, indicating that both share the same spirit.

But he who is joined to the Lord is one spirit with Him. 1 Corinthians 6:17

The Spirit of Christ has come to live within every born-again Christian. Your new spirit and the Spirit of Jesus have merged, have become one. So you are a completely new person. The identity and the holiness of your spirit is identical to that of Jesus.

It is no longer I who live, but Christ lives in me; and the life which I now live in the flesh I live by faith in the Son of God. Galatians 2:20
For who has known the mind of the Lord that he may instruct Him? But we have the mind of Christ. 1 Corinthians 2:16

The Scriptures say that your new spirit, in this world, is:

  • as perfect,
  • as mature,
  • as complete as Jesus is.
Because as He [Jesus] is, so are we in this world. 1 John 4:17
For by one offering He has perfected forever those who are being sanctified. Hebrews 10:14

Are we like Jesus in this world?

  • Not in your body (physical actions).
  • Not in your soul (thoughts, intellect, will).
  • In your spirit? YES! (by process of elimination)

No believer can claim that in his flesh he is like Jesus. But in his spirit, YES! Your spirit was:

  • created anew,
  • completely new in righteousness and holiness,
  • set apart (you have access to the Father and to spiritual truths).
You have come to Mount Zion… to the general assembly and church of the firstborn who are registered in heaven, to God the Judge of all, to the spirits of just men made perfect. Hebrews 12:22-23

D.4 Your new nature

You have a new nature created by God; not from your parents, not from Adam, but identical to the nature of Christ.

Be renewed in the spirit of your mind, and put on the new man [new spiritual nature], which was created according to God, in true righteousness and holiness. Ephesians 4:23-24

God sent the Holy Spirit, who dwells in us

Or do you not know that your body is the temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you, whom you have from God, and you are not your own? 1 Corinthians 6:19
By this we know that we abide in Him, and He in us, because He has given us of His Spirit. 1 John 4:13
And because you are sons, God has sent forth the Spirit of His Son into your hearts, crying out, "Abba, Father!" Galatians 4:6
Your new spirit CANNOT sin …because it was redeemed and sealed with the Holy Spirit.
In Him you also trusted, after you heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation; in whom also, having believed, you were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise, who is the guarantee of our inheritance until the redemption of the purchased possession, to the praise of His glory. Ephesians 1:13-14
Whoever has been born of God does not sin, for His seed remains in him; and he cannot sin, because he has been born of God. 1 John 3:9

D.5 What part of you died?

Your old spirit… no longer exists!
Knowing this, that our old man was crucified with Him, that the body of sin might be done away with, that we should no longer be slaves of sin. Romans 6:6
How shall we who died to sin live any longer in it (in sin)? Romans 6:2

There was a real death in you (your old spirit died, your old man).

I have been crucified with Christ; it is no longer I who live, but Christ lives in me; and the life which I now live in the flesh I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave Himself for me. Galatians 2:20

We died with Christ and rose again through baptism.

Therefore we were buried with Him through baptism into death, that just as Christ was raised from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life. Romans 6:4
But you are not in the flesh (soul and body) but in the Spirit, if indeed the Spirit of God dwells in you. Now if anyone does not have the Spirit of Christ, he is not His. And if Christ is in you, the body is dead because of sin, but the Spirit is life because of righteousness. Romans 8:9-10

How can we worship God? How can God have fellowship with an unholy man? What if we still do not reach God's standards to worship Him?

If we think deeply about it, we could not come near to God, unless we worship Him in spirit and in truth.

God is Spirit, and those who worship Him must worship in spirit and truth. John 4:24
Watch and pray, lest you enter into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak. Matthew 26:41

E Righteousness and no condemnation

E.1 Jesus put His righteousness in us

Because you put your faith in Jesus:

  • you received a new spirit,
  • becoming a completely new creature.

Your spirit is as righteous, holy, and pure as Jesus. It is His righteousness that was given to you (it did not come from you).

But of Him you are in Christ Jesus, who became for us from God wisdom and righteousness and sanctification and redemption. 1 Corinthians 1:30
❱ "Our old man was crucified with Him, that the body of sin might be done away with…" — see Romans 6:6 (Part D.5)

E.2 There is no condemnation from God

All punishment fell upon Christ on the cross.

There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus, who do not walk according to the flesh, but according to the Spirit. Romans 8:1
Blessed are those whose lawless deeds are forgiven, and whose sins are covered. Blessed is the man to whom the Lord shall not impute sin. Romans 4:7-8
And He Himself is the propitiation for our sins, and not for ours only but also for the whole world. 1 John 2:2
But God demonstrates His own love toward us, in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us. Much more then, having now been justified by His blood, we shall be saved from wrath through Him. Romans 5:8-9
Christ was offered once to bear the sins of many. Hebrews 9:28

Jesus not only freed you from sin and from your sins up to the moment of your salvation, but freed you from all your sins: past, present, and future.

Otherwise, from the moment of your salvation onward, it would be your own responsibility to free yourself from condemnation —by your own works— or to keep your salvation.

For he who has entered His rest has himself also ceased from his works as God did from His. Hebrews 4:10
Who gave Himself for our sins, that He might deliver us from this present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father. Galatians 1:4

E.3 If the believer sins, condemnation comes from other sources

If the believer sins, he sins in the flesh; but the flesh was already condemned and died together with Christ, paying for the condemnation of sin.

If the believer sins, he sins in the flesh; God no longer condemns us, for Christ took the blame, the condemnation, and died. So the condemnation will come from other sources, but it does not come from God.

We died and were raised together with Christ Jesus. Therefore, the penalty paid, the accused can no longer be condemned again.

  • Whoever breaks the laws of the world will be condemned by the law of the authorities.
  • Christians who walk "according to" the flesh can be condemned by their own consciences, by the accusations of the devil, and by the consequences of sin.
  • But there is no condemnation from God.
For the eyes of the Lord are on the righteous, and His ears are open to their prayers; but the face of the Lord is against those who do evil. And who is he who will harm you if you become followers of what is good? 1 Peter 3:12-13

Although the Bible does contemplate unjust persecution:

But even if you should suffer for righteousness' sake, you are blessed. And do not be afraid of their threats, nor be troubled, but sanctify the Lord God in your hearts, and always be ready to give a defense to everyone who asks you a reason for the hope that is in you, with meekness and fear. 1 Peter 3:14-15
For it is better, if it is the will of God, to suffer for doing good than for doing evil. 1 Peter 3:17

E.4 The balance of the heart

God is not the one who changes His heart toward me; rather, it is sin that hardens my heart toward God.

Beware, brethren, lest there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief in departing from the living God… lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin. Hebrews 3:12-13
There is a balance between…
  • (a) God's unconditional love, which through the sacrifice of Jesus does not condemn us;
  • (b) but a heart hardened toward God, and toward the things of God, affects my sensitivity and my relationship "toward" God, and my ability to receive what God has already given me.

E.5 Does this give us a right to sin?

What then? Shall we sin because we are not under the law but under grace? Certainly not! Romans 6:15
And having been set free from sin, you became slaves of righteousness. Romans 6:18

F Consequences of not understanding this truth

…about "spirit, soul and body."

F.1 Symptoms and frustrations in the Christian life

  • It can bring frustration and thoughts of condemnation.
  • It can create a great problem in your Christian life.

Have you felt any of this?

  • Do you see no change in your old habits?
  • Are you stuck in your past and your failures?
  • A lack of revelation of the Word? Is it hard for you to believe the Word?
  • Do you feel condemned by what you did, do, or will do?
  • Are you dominated by the weaknesses of the flesh, instead of enjoying your new life and the blessings of God?
  • Do God's blessings not flow in your life?
  • Are you praying, reading the Word, and listening to sermons without a real change?

F.2 What this prevents

This will prevent:

  • you from receiving the life that God already placed in your spirit,
  • the life and power from flowing from your spirit toward your soul and your body,
  • you from having fellowship with the Holy Spirit,
  • you from being transformed, because your flesh (soul and body) remains the same.

Then confusion, frustration, and unbelief come to make you give up or be discouraged from trying to live and enjoy a Christian life.

F.3 Faith and unbelief at the same time

Look at what Paul says regarding your flesh and your spirit.

I want to do what is good (spirit), but I don't. I don't want to do what is wrong, but I do it anyway (flesh). But if I do what I don't want to do, I am not really the one doing wrong; it is sin living in me that does it (flesh). I have discovered this principle of life—that when I want to do what is right, I inevitably do what is wrong. I love God's law with all my heart (spirit). But there is another power within me that is at war with my mind. This power makes me a slave to the sin that is still within me (flesh). Oh, what a miserable person I am! Who will free me from this life that is dominated by sin and death? Thank God! The answer is in Jesus Christ our Lord. So you see how it is: In my mind I really want to obey God's law, but because of my sinful nature I am a slave to sin. Romans 7:19-25 — NLT

For this reason we can have faith (in the spirit — Galatians 5:22) and unbelief (in the intellect), at the same time.

Jesus said to him, "If you can believe, all things are possible to him who believes." Immediately the father of the child cried out and said with tears, "Lord, I believe; help my unbelief!" Mark 9:23-24

G How to receive what is already in your spirit

G.1 Bring the soul into agreement with the spirit

For though we walk in the flesh, we do not war according to the flesh. For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal but mighty in God for pulling down strongholds, casting down arguments and every high thing that exalts itself against the knowledge of God, bringing every thought into captivity to the obedience of Christ, and being ready to punish all disobedience when your obedience is fulfilled. 2 Corinthians 10:3-6

Bring your soul into agreement with what is already in your spirit; then you will obtain the physical benefit in your soul and body.

[God] has blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places in Christ. Ephesians 1:3

Your soul must allow your spirit to flow toward it and toward your body, and so receive the power and life of God and the spiritual blessings He already deposited in you.

Let the Spirit renew your thoughts and attitudes. Put on your new nature, created to be like God — truly righteous and holy. Ephesians 4:23-24 — NLT

G.2 Your soul has a valve

That is the function of your natural mind (your mental-emotional part): to be transformed, allowing the life and power of God to flow from your spirit toward your soul and your body.

Beloved, I pray that you may prosper in all things and be in health, just as your soul prospers. 3 John 1:2

If your mind, which is a valve, is CLOSED:

  • it does not embrace this truth to renew your mind,
  • you do not obtain what you see in the spiritual mirror of God's Word,
  • then your physical mirror will be your reality (5 senses), instead of God's Word (the spiritual mirror),
  • then the resurrection life in your spirit is completely blocked.

The result is that in your mind you conclude:

  • I feel sick,
  • my body hurts,
  • I am dying,
  • I am depressed,
  • I am afraid,
  • your medical history dominates you,
  • you will not receive a single drop of God's life-giving power,
  • then you can live your life sick and die sick, even while having the power and life of God within you.

You can come to have depression, anger, bitterness, and fear, while all the time in your spirit you have love, joy, and peace…

But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, forbearance, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control. Against such things there is no law. Galatians 5:22-23 — NIV

The Holy Spirit who dwells in your spirit produces fruit (fruit in the singular: it produces all 9 at once). By ourselves we can produce nothing.

He who abides in Me, and I in him, bears much fruit; for without Me you can do nothing. John 15:5

By opening the eyes of your understanding, you will grow in the knowledge of Him, understand the reason for His calling, and receive the riches of His inheritance.

The God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give to you the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of Him, the eyes of your understanding being enlightened; that you may know what is the hope of His calling, what are the riches of the glory of His inheritance in the saints. Ephesians 1:17-18

The verse continues, explaining that the same power by which God raised Christ Jesus from death is already available in our spirit, for us who believe.

And what is the exceeding greatness of His power toward us who believe, according to the working of His mighty power which He worked in Christ when He raised Him from the dead and seated Him at His right hand in the heavenly places. Ephesians 1:19-20

G.3 How do we receive the fruit of the Spirit? Renewing the mind

The rest of your Christian life is about renewing the mind: opening the valve from the spirit toward the soul and body. So, when we do that, our body and soul will experience the benefit.

Present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable to God, which is your reasonable service. And do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind (opening the "valve"), that you may prove what is that good and acceptable and perfect will of God. Romans 12:1-2

G.4 "Conformed" to the world or "transformed" by the Word?

The word CONFORM (to mold) means to be formed or molded according to the world. You may receive pressure and knowledge from:

  • the world,
  • the devil,
  • individuals, teachers, family,
  • groups, news, media, schools,
  • unbelievers,
  • circumstances,
  • erroneous theories of science and false religions,
  • mistaken ideas of culture.

You came into this world innocent, but you will choose who will mold you: the world or the Word of God.

TRANSFORM = metamorphosis The word TRANSFORM is the word metamorphosis: the process by which a small worm becomes a butterfly.

If you desire that kind of transformation in your physical and emotional realm —changing from the bitter, wounded, sick, or defeated person into a victorious person— God wants you to be…

Transformed by the renewing of your mind (by the Word of God). Romans 12:2

The Word of God teaches you:

  • all spiritual truth,
  • your new values,
  • your new attitudes,
  • your true identity.

You will move from CONFORMING to TRANSFORMING into what the Word of God says about you; that is, who you really are.

G.5 Digesting the Word of God

The KNOWLEDGE of the Word of God is fundamental in this process. But besides knowledge, you must have:

  • the understanding of it,
  • and its application.
Knowledge is like bringing food to your mouth (the first step)
  • but you need to swallow that food
  • so that your body digests it,
  • and so the nutrients and benefits are released throughout your whole body.

The knowledge of the Word of God is important, but ONLY if you begin to understand it, to study it, and to meditate on it, so that you can release the life that is in it.

You should no longer walk as the rest of the Gentiles walk, in the futility of their mind (soul, flesh), having their understanding darkened, being alienated (excluded) from the life of God, because of the ignorance that is in them, because of the blindness of their heart. Ephesians 4:17-18
Two paths

You can choose to fight the battle yourself, trying to fight against your imperfections, focusing your mind on how to avoid doing them, and then falling and repenting each time you fail, and then repeating the cycle, to live under condemnation.

Or instead, focus on who you already are in your spirit (identity): a child of God, a new creature, led by the Holy Spirit, so that the power and life already in you flow toward your soul and your body, thus living a victorious life in Christ Jesus.

As you become sensitive to the Spirit, He will guide you in what to do, where to go, when, and how. It is not a continual mental struggle against your own flesh, but a flowing of the guidance and power that the Holy Spirit puts in your spirit.

The results you will obtain only the Holy Spirit knows, but they are always good, perfect, and acceptable (Romans 12:2).

But if your guide is your mind, your intellect, and your emotions, seeking what is best by yourself, or what is most logical in your view, the result is death:

I am the vine, you are the branches. He who abides in Me, and I in him, bears much fruit; for without Me you can do nothing. John 15:5

G.6 Think spiritually, not carnally

The Scriptures say we should NOT walk:

  • like a person who has no relationship with God,
  • like a Gentile or an unbeliever.
For to be carnally minded is death, but to be spiritually minded is life and peace. Romans 8:6

Carnal does not necessarily mean sin, but all sin is carnal. Carnal means living from the 5 senses, dominated by what you can:

  • see,
  • hear,
  • smell,
  • taste,
  • feel.
For those who live according to the flesh set their minds on the things of the flesh, but those who live according to the Spirit, the things of the Spirit. Romans 8:5
For if you live according to the flesh you will die; but if by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you will live. Romans 8:13
Therefore do not let sin reign in your mortal body (flesh), that you should obey it in its lusts. Romans 6:12

You can perceive reality beyond the flesh. Your soul, through faith, can believe in the Word of God. You can believe things you cannot see.

Like a mathematical formula
  • spiritual mindset  =  life + peace
  • carnal mindset  =  death (not only physical)

Death is the result of sin, which manifests in:

  • emotional sadness,
  • loneliness,
  • bitterness,
  • anger,
  • fear,
  • and finally death.

If you walk like an unbeliever (Gentile):

  • then you are NOT receiving spiritual truths in your mind,
  • but will be dominated by the natural.
You should no longer walk as the rest of the Gentiles, in the futility of their mind, having their understanding darkened, being alienated (excluded) from the life of God, because of the ignorance that is in them, because of the hardness of their heart. Ephesians 4:17-18
Born-again people cannot be "in" the flesh, but they can walk "according to" the flesh.

G.7 Transforming faith

That the sharing (koinōnia: participation, communication, benefit) of your faith may become effective (to work, be productive, change, transformation) by the acknowledgment (to understand, digest) of every good thing which is in you (in your spirit) in Christ Jesus. Philemon 1:6

This Scripture does NOT say:

  • "by" gaining more faith,
  • "by" going to God to ask for new anointing,
  • "by" asking for a double portion,
  • "by" obtaining more from God.

God has already given it to you, He has already placed it in your spirit; it says it is already in you: "is in you."

The understanding of "spirit, soul and body":

  • is not a matter of a moment,
  • nor is it a single thing that will change your life in an instant,
  • but yes, understanding it will begin the process.

It is such a radical thought that:

  • it will take you through a process of transformation,
  • that will not only benefit you, but also other people,
  • it will give you a different attitude,
  • confidence,
  • and the assurance to face problems and overcome them.

And you have the potential to believe it:

…be transformed by the renewing of your mind, that you may prove what is that good and acceptable and perfect will of God. Romans 12:1-2

✦ A process, not a moment

In this way you will begin to see the perfection that is already in your spirit from the day of your salvation.
